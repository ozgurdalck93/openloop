# OPENLOOP — UX Flow v2

## Screen 1 — Home

Header:
**Open Loops**

Top summary example:
**2 need you · 3 are waiting · 1 is coming up**

Sections:

### NEEDS YOU
Things where the next action belongs to the user.

### WAITING
Things dependent on another person/company/system.

### COMING UP
Future events and expected outcomes.

### BRING BACK LATER
Things intentionally parked for later.

Primary CTA:
**Tell me what’s going on**

Empty state:
**Nothing is asking you to keep it in your head right now.**

Secondary:
**Tell me a task, something you're waiting for, or something you want brought back later.**

## Tone

Calm.
Brief.
Contextual.
No guilt.
No productivity-bro language.
No “crush your goals”.
No streak punishment.

## Visual direction

- warm off-white base
- dark ink typography
- one restrained natural accent
- subtle borders
- generous whitespace
- rounded, but not bubbly
- no neon
- no AI-purple cliché
- no glassmorphism overload
- no dense dashboard
